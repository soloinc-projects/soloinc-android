Privacy policy

SoloINC for Android, from 8086 LLC. Last updated 30 October 2026.

In short: there is no SoloINC account, and nothing the app holds is sent to us. We run no server the app talks to, and the app has no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your phone

  • A device key that signs each of your answers. It is made in the phone's hardware keystore and never leaves it.
  • When paired with your Mac: the pairing token and your Mac's network addresses, kept encrypted on the phone.
  • Your company's asks, cards and views, cached on the phone so the Desk opens without waiting.
  • When the company runs on this phone: your model provider key, kept in the phone's keystore, and the company itself (its jobs and record).
  • The app's own event notes (for example "pairing completed", "answer sent from a notification"). They stay on the phone; when paired, they are added to your own Mac's record.

Where data goes

  • With your Mac: the phone talks only to SoloINC on your own Mac, over your own network. Your asks and answers travel between the two, encrypted in transit.
  • On this phone: agents send their work to your own model provider, with your own key. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives.
  • Google Play: buying or restoring Pro goes through Google Play Billing. The app also reads Google Play's age signal at first launch where a law requires it, and may ask Google Play to show its rating prompt. Google handles those under its own policy.
  • We, 8086 LLC, receive none of it.

What is never collected

No analytics or crash-reporting service, no advertising ID, no location, contacts, photos, health data or browsing history. Your fingerprint or face is checked by Android; the app learns only whether the check passed.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing code your Mac shows.
  • Notifications: to show asks that wait on you, with Approve and Decline.
  • Biometric: to confirm each answer with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Foreground service: only when you run the company on this phone, so it keeps working while the app is in the background.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and we hold nothing about you. In Settings, "Unpair this phone" makes the phone forget its token at once; your company stays on your Mac. Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on the phone, your model key included.

Children

SoloINC is a business tool, not made for children.

Contact

Questions: support@8086.llc. If this policy changes, this page changes with it and shows the new date.
