Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Say which phone and Android version you use, and whether the app runs with your Mac or on the phone.

First questions

How do I pair the phone with my Mac?
On your Mac, open SoloINC and choose Pair a phone. It shows a code that works once, for ten minutes. In the app, tap "Pair with my Mac" and scan it, or paste it.
The app says my Mac did not answer.
Check that SoloINC is open on the Mac and that both are on your own network, then tap Try again. Answers you give meanwhile wait as "Waiting to send" and go when the Mac is in reach.
Can I use it without a Mac?
Yes. Tap "Run it on this phone" and paste your own model key. The company then runs inside the app. A step that only a computer can do is marked "Needs a computer".
Why did an ask reach me late?
While the app is closed it checks for new asks every 15 minutes, so an ask can arrive up to 15 minutes late.
Why did several asks come as one notification?
Asks from agents your company marks low risk, arriving within 15 minutes, are grouped. Each is still its own answer. Turn grouping off in Settings › Notifications.
How do I approve from the lock screen?
Tap Approve or Decline on the notification and confirm with your fingerprint, face or PIN. Turn it off in Settings › Notifications.
What is a run, and what does Pro add?
A run is one agent doing one step of one job. Free gives 30 runs a month, 60 in your first month. Pro gives unlimited runs. Answering, the Desk, the record, Stop and Today never need Pro.
How do I cancel Pro or get a refund?
Cancel any time in Google Play › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's policy: Get refunds on Google Play. To restore Pro on a new phone, open Settings › Plan › See Pro › Restore purchase.
How do I unpair or remove my data?
Settings › Unpair this phone. Uninstalling removes everything the app kept on the phone. See the privacy policy.
