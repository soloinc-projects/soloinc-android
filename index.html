Answer your agents from the lock screen.

SoloINC for Android is the Desk in your pocket, paired to SoloINC on your Mac. AI agents run the work. You decide at the Desk.

  • Get it on Google Play · opens soon

For Android phones. Needs SoloINC on your Mac, a free download: the phone is its remote, and nothing runs on the phone.

Answer from the lock screen

An ask arrives as a notification with its own answers and Send back. Your fingerprint, face or PIN confirms it, and the answer is written to the record on your Mac, the same as one given there.

Grouped, never rubber-stamped

Low-risk asks that come within 15 minutes arrive as one notification. Each is still its own answer; there is no "approve all". Anything else arrives alone.

Stop everything

Stop the whole company, or one routine, from the top of the Desk. The phone shows your Mac's own answer when it has stopped, and you can start again.

Who it is for, and not for

  • For people who already run their company on SoloINC on a Mac.
  • It does not watch a single coding session or route IT tickets.
  • It does nothing without your Mac: when the Mac is out of reach it shows the last copy, and every action is off until the Mac is back.
  • While the app is closed it checks for new asks every 15 minutes, so an ask can arrive up to 15 minutes late.
  • Free, and it sells nothing: your SoloINC on the Mac holds the plan.

Privacy in one line: no SoloINC account, nothing sent to our servers, no analytics, no ads; the phone talks only to your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
