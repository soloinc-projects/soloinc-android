Answer your agents from the lock screen.

SoloINC for Android runs your company's Desk from your phone: with your Mac, or with no Mac at all. AI agents run the work. You decide at the Desk.

  • Get it on Google Play · opens soon

For Android phones. No SoloINC account, no analytics, no ads: the phone talks only to your Mac, or to your own model provider.

Approve from the lock screen

An ask arrives as a notification with Approve and Decline. Your fingerprint, face or PIN confirms it, and the answer writes the same record line as one given on your Mac.

Grouped, never rubber-stamped

Two or more low-risk asks within 15 minutes come as one notification. Each is still its own answer; there is no "approve all". Anything else arrives alone, at once.

With no Mac at all

Choose "Run it on this phone" and the same engine runs inside the app on your own model key, kept in the phone's keystore. A step only a computer can do says "Needs a computer".

Free, and SoloINC Pro

Free

30 runs a month, 60 in your first month. A run is one agent doing one step of one job. The Desk, answering, the record, Stop and Today never need Pro.

SoloINC Pro

Unlimited runs: $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the U.S., paid through Google Play. It renews until you cancel; cancel any time in Google Play.

Who it is for, and not for

  • For people who already run their company on SoloINC.
  • It does not watch a single coding session or route IT tickets.
  • It includes no model: running on the phone needs your own model key. Pairing needs SoloINC on your Mac.
  • While the app is closed it checks for new asks every 15 minutes, so an ask can arrive up to 15 minutes late.

Privacy in one line: no SoloINC account, nothing sent to our servers, no analytics, no ads. Read the privacy policy.
